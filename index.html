SQUID DICE WARNING

Playing as a Guest means all achievements and records in this mode will not be saved. All progress will be lost once the player logs out.

Achievements and records that will not be saved include:
• Coins acquired
• Levels unlocked
• Game history

Online gaming is not available in this mode.

SQUID DICE SHORTCUTS FOR PC USERS

Here are some keys you can use in the game:

  • Press -C- to change the color at random.
  • Press -P- to play a random sound.
  • Press -K- to open or close the information panel.
  • Press -R- to roll your dice when it’s your turn in a match.
  • Press -S- to save your record while it’s your turn in a match.

If this is your first time playing, try watching the How to Play video and reading the notes.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT THE GAME

Introduction: This game is about rolling a die and trying to score as many points as possible without rolling a 1. The goal of the game is to reach 100 points. The first player to reach 100 points is declared the Winner.

  • You can play with a friend.
  • You can play against the AI, which I created specially for you and unlock levels from Easy → Master.

Usage

  • Check the game features in Settings.
  • To view this information again, click on About (above the refresh icon at the bottom right) or press K.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Scan the QR code to open the game on your device.
